ヒトリ妻

ドラマ日常青春

あらすじ・作品紹介

Here we go again… Yukina had come to visit her husband, who’d been injured at work, but she was dumbfounded to see him striking up conversations with every woman in sight. She ignored him and sat down on a bench in the hallway. Then, a cup of coffee was handed to her. It was from Yuto, a young man visiting his girlfriend—whom he’d met recently—and who was doting on her with a friendly, refreshing charm. For Yukina, who couldn’t help but compare him to her husband, it was just a distraction—perfect for simply watching—and she was supposed to be savoring that moment… <br><br> After seeing a flyer for a part-time job at a newly opened café, she rushed in. Standing across from her at the counter was Yuto, the man she’d met at the hospital. She was hired on the spot, and at the newly opened shop, it was just her and Yuto, the owner… For this married woman, who had devoted herself entirely to her husband, this new encounter marked the beginning of a daily life steeped in transgression—lewd, maddening, and utterly intoxicating… <br><br> (Source: Getchu, translated)
