あらすじ・作品紹介

After entering college, Azusa Shirakawa was enjoying campus life, marking a fresh start from her high school days, when she had devoted herself entirely to judo. But a casual joke from her childhood friend, Sae, ignited a fire within her. <i>“Having a friends-with-benefits relationship isn't so bad, you know?”</i> She had brushed off those words as a joke, but—her “one-time fling” with Takuma, her serious and sincere childhood friend, awakened a desire within Azusa, and her body found itself unable to resist the pleasure. Contrary to her intentions, she found herself losing control time and time again. <i>“I never thought the day would come when I'd be getting my body in shape not for judo competitions, but for sex...”</i> She, who was supposed to be so strong-willed, finds herself “losing” to a man. He—serious yet lecherous—begins to “corrupt” her. <br><br> (Source: Pinkpineapple, translated)
