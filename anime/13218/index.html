あらすじ・作品紹介

Based on the erotic game by Studio Pork. <br><br> Sunao Saiki, who had just moved into an apartment, was a student preparing for college entrance exams. He began his new life living alone with a somber expression. However, after meeting the wife of the couple next door and being captivated by her beauty, Sunao’s new life began to brighten up. <i>“I’m Ayano Shinonome, your neighbor. Nice to meet you.”</i> By chance, Sunao ended up working at the same part-time job as Ayano. As Ayano kindly looked out for him both at work and in the apartment, Sunao gradually found himself drawn to her. But she was a married woman. Sunao tried to hold back, yet his feelings were impossible to suppress, and he spent his days torn by inner conflict. At the same time, Ayano, too, began to see the likeness of her first love in Sunao and started to view him as a man… The memory of a first love, the warmth of a married woman. Separated by just a single wall, a dangerous romance begins to blossom. <br><br> (Source: Getchu, translated)
